# Create photos and videos

Photo Room turns a supported 3D project into a composed image or short model animation on your phone. Rendering happens in the app; Volum does not send the model to a cloud rendering service.

## Open Photo Room

Open a project and tap **Photo Room** beside **View in 3D**. You can also open Photo Room while previewing a local model before saving it to Volum.

## Choose the output format

Tap **Format** in the bottom toolbar, then choose:

- **Square** — 1:1 at 1080 × 1080;
- **Portrait** — 4:5 at 1080 × 1350;
- **Landscape** — 1.91:1 at 1080 × 566;
- **Reel / Story** — 9:16 at 1080 × 1920.

The format chip beneath the canvas is another shortcut to the same chooser. Reel / Story also shows a safe area to help keep important content away from common interface overlays.

## Compose the scene

Use the remaining toolbar controls to prepare the shot:

- **Scene** selects a solid color, gradient, or photo background.
- **Model** changes the model position, size, view, turn, and tilt.
- **Text** adds movable, resizable, and rotatable titles or callouts.
- **Motion** creates a spin, flip, roll, or tumble animation and previews it before export.

The controls panel can be collapsed when you need more canvas space.

## Share or save

Tap **Done**, then:

- tap **Share photo** to render a JPEG and immediately open the phone's Share / Save sheet;
- tap **Render MP4** to explicitly render a silent 1080p H.264 video, then share or save it;
- when offered, add the rendered photo back to the project and optionally use it as the project cover.

Photo export is a single action: you do not need to prepare the image and then tap a second download button.

## If a model cannot open

Photo Room requires a model that the mobile 3D viewer can load. Large or unsupported viewer assets can still use the rest of the project detail screen, but Photo Room may be unavailable for that model.
